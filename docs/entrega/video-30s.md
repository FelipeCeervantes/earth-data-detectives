# 30-second video: technical demo

> **Finished video:** `docs/entrega/video-30s.mp4`, exactly 30.000 s (900 frames at 30 fps), 1920×1080, −16.3 LUFS.
> It is a technical demo of a research project, not an ad: what we measured, how we tested it and what we found.
> Made with HyperFrames (project in `video/`, plan in `video/SPEC.md`, scenes generated by `video/generar.py`) from screen
> recordings of the app. The narration is the Gemini "Puck" voice in a science-documentary tone; a Gemini transcription
> confirmed it matches the script word for word. No music, no NASA logos, no link card.
> The cursor in shot 2 is drawn on top, following the real recorded mouse path, so the hover and click effects are the app's own.

## Script (66 words) and shots

| Time | Narration (English) | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–5.6 | "Is Los Altos de Jalisco running out of water? We asked forty-four years of NASA data." | The 3D globe with the "Data loaded" marker; the question lands word by word; the years count 1981 → 2024 as the globe dives to Los Altos. |
| 5.6–9.4 | "Every municipality is colored by its own rainfall trend." | A cursor glides over the map: each municipality lights up and shows its name and trend; click, and the camera flies in. Legend: less water ↔ more water. |

| 15.2–22.0 | "So far, rainfall shows no clear trend, but soil moisture is declining, season by season." | "No clear trend" (p = 0.25) on the rainfall verdict; then the soil chart, "Declining by season" (seasonal test, p = 0.016). |
| 22.0–27.4 | "And if the past repeats, a drought year within the next five is ninety-five percent likely." | The drought chart; "11 of 24 years had drought" and 95% counting up. |
| 27.4–30.0 | — | The planet turning: "Steady rain. Drier soils, by season. Recurring drought." Data: NASA POWER (MERRA-2) · CONAGUA · INEGI. |

**Accuracy notes.**
- "No clear trend" means both tests give p > 0.05 for rainfall, 2001–2024.
- The soil decline comes from the seasonal Mann-Kendall test on root-zone moisture (p = 0.016). The overall trend is not yet significant, which is why the line says "by season".
- "A drought year" means a year with at least one month at SPI-6 of −1 or below. That happened in 11 of 24 years, so the chance in five years is 1 − (1 − 0.46)⁵ ≈ 95% if the past repeats. It is a frequency, not a forecast.
- r = 0.96 is the year-to-year correlation between NASA POWER and the 20 CONAGUA rain gauges.
