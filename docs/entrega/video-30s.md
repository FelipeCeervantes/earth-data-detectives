# 30-second video: script and shot list

> Narration in English (official). The Spanish translation below is only for the team to review. **[TEAM]** is the team name as registered.
> Every number matches the app (NASA POWER, 2001–2024). No NASA logos, flags or mission identifiers. Minors do not appear on camera and use a voice modulator (an AI voice also works).

## Script (≈ 84 words, 30 s)

| Time | Narration (English) | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | "Hi, we're **[TEAM]**, and this is Earth Data Detectives." | Title card: project name, slogan "Is Los Altos running out of water? Ask the data.", team name, and the challenge title as it appears on the Space Apps site. |
| 4–8 s | "In Los Altos de Jalisco, Mexico, people ask: are we running out of water?" | The app's **Where?** map: outline of the 20 municipalities. Caption: "Los Altos de Jalisco, Mexico · 20 municipalities". |
| 8–17 s | "With free NASA POWER data, the app compares rain and soil moisture with the normal, measures drought and soil stress, and tests every trend." | Screen recording: choose zone and period in the detective panel → **Rainfall** tab (verdict stamp) → **Meteorological drought** bars → **Soil moisture**. Caption: "Data: NASA POWER (MERRA-2) · 1981–2024". |
| 17–21 s | "Gemini explains it all in plain words, without inventing numbers." | **Summary** tab, highlight "Written by Gemini… (checked: no new numbers)". |
| 21–26 s | "The worst drought hit in 2011, and soil moisture shows signs of decline in the south." | Drought chart zoomed on Jan 2011 – Jan 2012 ("Extreme drought"), then the map with **Altos Sur** selected. |
| 26–30 s | "Next: more regions and early drought warnings. NASA science, for everyone." | Closing card: "Next: more regions · early drought warnings", slogan and the link to the live app. |

**If it runs long**, cut ", and tests every trend" first, then "Mexico,". Read at an energetic pace: about 2.8 words per second.

## Production notes
- **Narration:** an energetic voice with clear pauses. It can be generated with an AI voice (useful if any team member is under 18).
- **Captions:** burn in English captions with the same text (accessibility, and many people watch without sound).
- **Images:** only our own app screens and our own footage. No stock photos or music without a license; if there is music, use a royalty-free track and credit it.
- **Accuracy:** "signs of decline in the south" refers to the seasonal Mann-Kendall test in Altos Sur (p = 0.001); the overall regression is not significant yet. Do not say "drying out" or name causes.
- **Format:** 1920×1080, 30 s or less, MP4. Upload it where it can be viewed publicly (for example YouTube, unlisted) and put the link on the project page.
